**Conchilla 0.5.0**: Conchilla on Android

- **New: Conchilla for Android** (`Conchilla-0.5.0.apk` below). Join a room with the code and watch on your phone:
  - Pinch to zoom, drag to move the picture, tap for the controls, double-tap for fullscreen (landscape).
  - Chat pops up over the stream as holocall cards; tap 💬 to type.
  - Volume up to 200%, Auto delay buffer, and the screen stays on while you're in a room.
  - Hosting stays on the PC app.
  - Updates: **Check for updates** in the app downloads the newest APK.
- **Chat from your phone while your laptop watches:** in the lobby (or 📱 while watching) press **📱 Phone**, then put the 8-letter code into JOIN on the phone. The phone chats **as you** (your name and colour), through your laptop. It doesn't take a viewer spot and works in sealed rooms.
- **Chat only:** tick *Chat only* before joining to be in the room just for chat (you can still tap *Watch here too*).
- PC app: no changes besides touch-screen support while watching.

**Installing the APK:** download it on the phone, open it, and allow installing from your browser when Android asks.
