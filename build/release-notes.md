**Conchilla 0.1.0**: first release 🐭💠

Easy screen sharing for your friend group, plus a neon chat that floats over your games.

- **Pick a name + face, then Host or Join with a 6-letter code.** Everyone in a room gets their own colour.
- **Direct connection**: your picture goes straight from the host's PC to each friend, with no server in between. Up to native resolution, 30–120 fps, H.264 / AV1 / VP9 / VP8, game sound in stereo up to 510 kbps.
- **Ultrawide**: the stream keeps the host's real shape (21:9 / 32:9). Viewers can **zoom / crop** to cut black bars (slider, scroll wheel, drag to move, *Fill* / *Fit*).
- **Delay buffer** with a live meter: shows what % of frames arrive on time and how much smoother the buffer makes it. *Auto* picks one for you.
- **Host controls**: kick, block, 🔒 lock joins, max people, and every stream setting. Each person's live quality is shown.
- **In-game chat**: press **Caps Lock** in any game, type, press Enter, and you're back in the game. Messages pop up like a Cyberpunk holocall in the corner and fade after 5 s. The chat window is pinned on top and click-through.
- **F7** shows or hides stream info. All hotkeys can be changed in Settings.
- When the host stops sharing, or closes the window being shared, everyone goes back to the lobby.

Games need **borderless / windowed fullscreen** for the overlay to show on top.
